ACUTE WEB / PRIVACY

Privacy is
the starting point.

A browser is a personal space. These are the principles behind Acute, with links to the current platform policies.

Your browsing isn’t our business.

Acute’s Android and iOS projects disable telemetry, diagnostic reporting and automatic crash uploads. The iOS project also declares no tracking or collected data in its privacy manifest.

Private browsing has a purpose—and limits.

On iOS, private tabs use non-persistent website storage and are excluded from history, saved sessions and closed-tab recovery. Private browsing does not make you anonymous to websites, your network provider or a search engine.

Built on established browser engines.

Android uses Mozilla’s Gecko engine. iPhone and iPad use WebKit, with web content kept in WebKit’s sandboxed content processes. The two apps share principles while retaining platform-specific implementations.

Read the platform policies.

For the current implementation details, read the Android privacy policy and iOS privacy policy.

Report a security concern.

Follow the Android project’s security policy for its reporting instructions. For iOS support and reporting information, visit the iOS repository.
